ARCHITECTURAL GENEALOGY

The Hashti (هشتی): The Sacred Spatial Threshold

In traditional Persian architecture — prominent across Isfahan, Yazd, Kashan, and Shiraz — the Hashti is the vaulted transitional vestibule positioned immediately behind the entrance portal (sar-dar). Derived from the Persian word for eight (hasht), it is an octagonal or polygonal vaulted chamber that serves as a sensory pause between the chaotic exterior street and the tranquil intimacy of the private home.

The Spatial Threshold Sequence

01
کوچه (Koucheh — The Public Street)
The open, bustling external street realm. High sensory stimuli, continuous movement, and social exposure.
02
سردر (Sar-Dar — The Entrance Portal)
The monumental pointed ogival arch that announces transition. Built with the classic 32° arch pitch mirrored in our logo.
03
هشتی (Hashti — The Vestibule)
The octagonal pause chamber. Natural cooling, zenithal skylight (hoor-noor), acoustic tranquility, and respectful orientation.
04
دالان (Dalan — The Indirect Passage)
A bent corridor intentionally blocking direct sightlines into the private dwelling to preserve privacy (mahramiyat).
05
حیاط (Hayat — The Courtyard Sanctuary)
The destination: lush central garden, fountain pool (howz), sunlight, and harmonious living spaces.

The Digital Metaphor

Just as the historical Hashti welcomes weary travelers into a calm domestic refuge, Hashti Design System serves as the welcoming architectural threshold between complex underlying application logic and intuitive human experience.

Mathematical Vector Geometry

The Hashti logo mark is constructed from 3 concentric arches featuring the authentic ~32° pitch characteristic of Safavid Persian masonry, resting upon an exact baseline with a central solid gateway anchor. Every stroke width (16px) and negative clearance gap (14px) adheres to a golden spatial cadence.

✦ Accessible
Engineered for WCAG 2.2 AAA rigor. 7:1 minimum contrast ratios, full keyboard parity, and native screen-reader ARIA semantics.
◈ Elegant
Grounded in pure monochrome restraint (#000000 & #FFFFFF), crisp geometric arches, and an uncompromising 8pt spatial grid.
⚡ Open Source
Modern TypeScript foundation, Bun runtime velocity, unbundled native ESM, zero tracking, and permissive MIT licensing.
⇄ International
First-class bidirectional symmetry. LTR and RTL are treated as design equals, supporting English, Persian, Arabic, and Hebrew.
