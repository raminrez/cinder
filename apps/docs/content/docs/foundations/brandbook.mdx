## Brand Voice & Tone

When writing documentation, interface copy, error messages, and release notes for Hashti, adhere to our four tonal tenets:

### 1. Architectural & Serene
- **Do:** Use dignified, clear, spatial language ("threshold", "elevation", "sanctuary", "surface", "pathway").
- **Don't:** Use hyperbolic tech jargon, aggressive slang, or overly playful colloquialisms.

### 2. Dignified Hospitality (*Ta'arof without Friction*)
- **Do:** Greet the user with warmth, quiet confidence, and helpful error recovery.
- **Don't:** Blame the user for errors or construct impenetrable walls of technical stack traces.

### 3. Precise & Mathematical
- **Do:** Quote exact WCAG contrast ratios (e.g. "7:1 contrast ratio"), stroke weights, and pixel measurements.
- **Don't:** Make vague claims like "it looks nice" or "accessible enough".

### 4. Truly Bilingual
- **Do:** Respect Persian and Arabic grammar, typography, and cultural nuance. When authoring RTL specimens, use meaningful literary and architectural vocabulary.
- **Don't:** Treat RTL as an automated translation afterthought or machine-flipped English copy.

## Usage Guidelines & Clear Space

To maintain the architectural dignity of the Hashti mark:

- **Minimum Clear Space:** Maintain a clear margin around the mark equal to the width of the central gateway arch on all sides.
- **Minimum Digital Size:** Do not render the primary logo lockup smaller than **24px** height in web interfaces or **16px** for standalone marks.
- **Do Not Distort:** Never stretch, skew, or alter the 32° arch pitch of the concentric vaults.
- **Do Not Colorize Arbitrarily:** Render the logo in obsidian black, chalk white, or our signature Persian Turquoise (`#0D9488`).
